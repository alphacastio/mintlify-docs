## Core concepts

| Concept | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Dataset](/concepts/datasets) | A structured table of data, usually organized by date and by entities such as country or category. |
| [Repository](/concepts/repositories) | The place where your work lives, and where you control who can access it. |
| [Pipeline](/steps/overview) | An ordered series of steps that turns source data into an output. |
| [Dashboard](/dashboards) | A page that groups charts, tables, and other views. |
| [Agent](/ai/agents) | An AI assistant with its own instructions and data access. |
