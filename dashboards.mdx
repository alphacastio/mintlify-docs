A dashboard puts related content on one page. It is saved in a [repository](/concepts/repositories), and the repository controls who can see it. Creating and cloning dashboards requires a [plan](https://www.alphacast.io/pricing) that lets you save content.

## Create a dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clip your content">
    Use **Clip** on the charts and other content you want to include. See [Clips](/clips).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the dashboard">
    Click **Create new**, then **Dashboard**. Type a title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add content">
    Drag items from your clips onto the page. Move and resize them on the grid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add text">
    Drag **Drag a new text box** onto the page and write your notes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Organize with sections

Click **Add Section** to group content under a heading. To show sections as tabs, open **Settings** in the editor and turn on **Display sections as tabs**.

## Add filters

Filters come from a deck, which a [Deck view step](/steps/deck-view) creates. Clip the deck, then drag its **Filters** item onto the dashboard along with the deck's charts or tables.

In the filter block, open **Edit Filters** to set:

- The block **Title**.
- **Reader controls**: **Show controls to readers** or **Apply defaults silently**. Hidden controls still filter the dashboard.
- Which columns readers can filter. Each filter has its own type, such as **Multi-select**, **Single select** or **Date range**, and a default.

If the dashboard uses more than one deck, you can turn on **Synchronized filters** in **Settings**. A selection in one deck then applies to other decks with matching column names.

## Dashboard settings

Open **Settings** in the editor to choose:

- The **Repository** where the dashboard is saved.
- **Display sections as tabs** and **Synchronized filters**.
- An **Excerpt** (a short summary) and **Tags**.

The editor's options menu includes **Copy Link**, **Undo last change** and **Delete** when they apply.

## Read, copy and export

Open the menu on a dashboard:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Share** | Copy or post the link, or get [embed code](/connect/embeds) |
| **Open in Fullscreen** | Use the whole screen |
| **Print** | Print the page |
| **Edit** | Change the dashboard. Requires write access |
| **Clone** | Make your own copy |
| **Clone current tab** | Copy one tab into another dashboard, or a new one |
| **Select charts** | Pick charts and download them as images |

The actions you see depend on the dashboard, your access and your plan. A repository owner can limit cloning to people with clone access.

A cloned tab keeps pointing to the original content. If the original is private, readers need access to it. See [public dashboards with private content](/sharing#public-dashboards-with-private-content).
